## 1. Who we are

**Durbaan** is a Somali impact communications and data studio.

**Tagline:** Your impact, heard.

**One line:** Documentaries, M&E dashboards, websites and systems for NGOs, programs and businesses across Somalia, Somaliland, Ethiopia and Kenya.

**The name:** *Durbaan* is the Somali word for drum. Before radios and phones, the drum called communities together to hear news that mattered: a gathering, a celebration, a warning, a decision. We do the same for organisations whose work deserves to be heard.

**Short bio (about 50 words):**
Durbaan helps NGOs, UN programs and businesses in the Horn of Africa show what they achieve. We make impact documentaries with Somali-speaking crews, build M&E dashboards and data systems, and design websites that earn trust. Every result we publish is confirmed. Based in Mogadishu and Nairobi.

**Key facts:**

- 4 countries: Somalia, Somaliland, Ethiopia and Kenya

- 2 bases: Mogadishu and Nairobi

## 4. How we work

1. **Listen.** A free 30 minute discovery call. We learn who needs to see your work, and what they need to believe.
2. **Plan.** A written proposal with three options, each with a fixed price, timeline and list of deliverables.
3. **Make.** Production with fixed check-ins. Drafts on agreed dates, with two rounds of changes built in.
4. **Launch and measure.** We launch with you, train your team, and report back on what the work achieved.

### What clients can hold us to
- **Prices in public.** Every package shows a starting price and what is included.
- **Fixed timelines.** Most packages take 3 to 8 weeks, with check-in dates agreed before we start.
- **Clear payment terms.** Under 5,000 USD: 50% to start, 50% on delivery. Larger projects: 40%, 30%, 30%.
- **You own the files.** Everything we make is yours once paid in full.
- **Consent in their language.** Every person we film gives informed consent in their own language. Donor safeguarding standards, always.
- **Local crews, donor standards.** Somali-speaking teams in the field, reporting in the format your donor requires.

## 6. Brand voice

**We sound like:** a calm, competent partner who has been in the field. Plain, specific and warm. Confident without boasting.

**Rules:**
- **No em dashes.** Use full stops, commas or colons instead.
- **Only real, confirmed results.** Never invent or round up numbers. If a number is not confirmed, describe the work instead.
- **Specific beats impressive.** "Five video stories against a minimum of three" beats "exceeded expectations".
- **Short sentences.** One idea per sentence. Write for people reading on a phone.
- **People first.** Name the community, the place and the change, not just the organisation.
- **Respect and dignity.** Never use pity imagery or language. People are partners and protagonists, not victims.
- **Consistent spelling.** Pick British or American spelling and keep it the same within a piece.

**Words we like:** visible, credible, proof, heard, clear, confirmed, on the ground, in their own language.
**Words we avoid:** "empower" as filler, "leverage", "synergy", "world-class", "cutting-edge", "game-changer", "revolutionary".

**Recurring lines:**
- Your impact, heard.
- Proof, not promises.
- Many strong organisations lose out because their work is invisible. Not weak. Invisible.
- Every result we publish is confirmed.

### Visual identity
- **Colours:** Navy #0E1F3D (primary), Amber #FFB020 (accent), Cream #F5E6C8, Night #081428, Slate #5B6475, Mist #F6F7F9
- **Fonts:** Unbounded (headings), Inter (body)
- **Logo:** the Durbaan seal (a drum inside a ring) with the lowercase "durbaan" wordmark. Use the light version on navy, the dark version on light backgrounds.
- **Photography:** real field photos, natural light, people with dignity and consent. No stock photos of Africa.

5. **Call to action** (1 line): "Book a free call at durbaan.com" or a question to the reader.
